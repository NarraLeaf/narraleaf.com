---
title: "EasingDefinition"
description: The union of named easing keywords, a `BezierDefinition`, and a `CustomEasingFunction` accepted wherever a transform needs an easing curve.
---
```typescript
type EasingDefinition = CustomEasingFunction | BezierDefinition | "linear" | "easeIn" | "easeOut" | "easeInOut" | "circIn" | "circOut" | "circInOut" | "backIn" | "backOut" | "backInOut" | "anticipate";
```

For CustomEasingFunction, see [CustomEasingFunction](/docs/narraleaf-react/core/types/CustomEasingFunction).  
For BezierDefinition, see [BezierDefinition](/docs/narraleaf-react/core/types/BezierDefinition).
